Player Data Field Guide

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Neuro Venture: ADHD Focus uses and how your data is handled. The short version: your quests and progress stay on your device, and you choose when to contact support.

Effective August 13, 2026
DataStorageApple ServicesYour ChoicesContact

Privacy at a Glance

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • Your task, progress, hero, and reward data are stored locally on your device.
  • Neuro Venture does not sell your personal information.
  • Support information is shared only when you choose to send an email.

1. Information the App Uses

Neuro Venture may store information you create in the app, including task and reminder text, optional notes, due dates, completion history, XP, Gold, selected hero, rewards, preferences, and active focus-timer information.

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Neuro Venture requests read-only access to today's step count. Step data is used to show Health-connected quest progress in the app and its widgets.

This information is used to provide the app's quest, habit, progress, reward, reminder, timer, and widget features.

2. Local Storage

Your gameplay and task data are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage systems. Neuro Venture does not require you to create an account and does not operate a developer-owned server for your quest data.

If you delete the app, reset the device, or erase its data, locally stored information may be lost. Device backups are controlled by your Apple and device settings.

3. Apple Services

Purchases: Premium Adventurer purchases and restorations are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy. Neuro Venture receives only the information needed to confirm your entitlement.

Notifications: If you enable notifications, the app uses Apple's notification system to deliver reminders and timer alerts. You can change permission at any time in iOS or iPadOS Settings.

Apple Health: Step access is optional and controlled through Health permissions. Neuro Venture does not write to Apple Health. Health data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with data brokers.

4. Support Emails

If you choose Send Feedback or Report a Problem, your mail app may prepare a message containing information you provide. A problem report may include app version, build, device type, operating-system version, or relevant reward transaction history so the issue can be investigated.

Email is sent only when you choose to send it. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in task names, notes, or support messages.

5. Sharing and Sale of Data

Neuro Venture does not sell your personal information. Information is not shared with advertisers. Data may be handled by Apple when you use App Store purchases, notifications, device backups, or other Apple services, subject to Apple's terms and policies.

6. Your Choices

  • Edit, complete, or delete tasks and rewards inside the app.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or your device settings.
  • Allow or revoke Apple Health step access in Health or device settings.
  • Choose whether to send feedback or problem reports.
  • Delete the app to remove its locally stored app data, subject to any device backups.

7. Children's Privacy

Neuro Venture is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Because task text is stored locally, parents and guardians should supervise use as appropriate for their household.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through your preferred mail app.
